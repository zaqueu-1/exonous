Restraint demands precision. Lower visual intensity on loud, aggressive, or overstimulating UI without erasing personality or sliding into generic.

---

## Visitor mode

**Persuade + Experience:** quieter → restrained palette, more whitespace, typographic air. Less drama, same POV.

**Operate + Read:** quieter → less visual noise. Fewer accents, flatter cards, less color and motion. The tool fades into the task.

---

## Assess

Find what drives intensity:

1. **Sources**
   - saturated or overly bright color;
   - extreme high-contrast pairings;
   - too many bold, heavy elements competing;
   - excess motion or dramatic animation;
   - decorative clutter, patterns, ornaments;
   - uniform large scale with weak hierarchy.

2. **Context**
   - purpose (marketing vs tool vs reading);
   - audience (some contexts need energy);
   - what already works;
   - core message to preserve.

If unclear from the codebase, STOP and AskUserQuestion.

**CRITICAL:** Quieter ≠ boring. Refined and easier on the eyes—luxury, not laziness.

## Plan

Strategy to reduce intensity while keeping impact:

- color: desaturate or shift to restrained tones?
- hierarchy: what stays bold (few), what recedes?
- simplification: what can go entirely?
- sophistication: quality through restraint?

Subtlety without intent becomes generic.

## Refine

### Color

- Saturation toward 70–85% vs full punch.
- Muted tones over bright primaries.
- Fewer hues, used deliberately.
- Neutrals carry weight; color as ~10% accent.
- High contrast only where it matters.
- Warm/cool tinted grays vs flat gray.
- On colored surfaces: darker shade of that hue or transparency—not gray-on-color.

### Weight

- Lighten weights (900→600, 700→500); shrink sizes where hierarchy allows.
- Hierarchy via weight, size, space—not only color/bold.
- More whitespace; less density.
- Thinner, softer, or removed borders.

### Simplification

- Drop purposeless gradients, shadows, patterns, textures.
- Moderate radius; simpler shapes.
- Flatter layering where possible.
- Reduce blur, glow, stacked shadows.

### Motion

- Shorter travel (10–20px vs 40px); gentler easing.
- Keep functional motion; drop decorative loops.
- Gentle micro-feedback vs dramatic effects.
- ease-out-quart for understated motion; avoid bounce/elastic.
- Remove motion with no clear job.

### Composition

- Smaller size jumps between levels.
- Grid-align strays.
- Even spacing rhythm vs wild swings.

**NEVER:** flat hierarchy; all-gray palette; zero personality; usability sacrificed; everything tiny and light.

## Verify

- Tasks still easy?
- Still distinctive?
- Better for extended reading?
- POV survives the cuts?

Then `/ui-design polish`.
