> **Prerequisite:** know the product’s allowed emotional range.

Earned personality at meaningful beats—not generic whimsy. Character shows up in useful interactions, humane copy, and considered details.

---

## Visitor mode

- **Persuade + Experience:** voice, layout, motion, discovery may carry character if the artifact stays central.
- **Operate + Read:** invest charm in first use, completion, recovery, mastery; elsewhere optimize reliability.

## Spot the beat

Review target, DESIGN.md, voice, repeat frequency, emotional stakes:

- effort deserving acknowledgment;
- waits that can inform;
- empty/first-use moments that orient;
- errors needing empathy;
- interactions whose feedback could feel on-brand;
- capabilities worth discovering.

Skip celebrations for routine clicks. Ask when emotional range or stakes are unknown.

## One-line thesis

State what the user should feel and why it fits this product. Pick the smallest delivery:

- distinctive feedback on meaningful action;
- clarifying copy with voice;
- motion/material with recognizable behavior;
- illustration, sound, haptic, or environmental detail rooted in the world;
- discovery that exposes real utility.

Derive from mechanism and world—not a template library.

## Moment-by-moment

- **Success:** scale to effort; big wins may expand; routine saves stay quiet and sure.
- **Waiting:** honest progress or context—never fake latency for show.
- **Empty/first use:** clarify next step before ornament.
- **Errors:** problem + path forward first; warmth OK; no jokes on money, privacy, loss, or blocked work.
- **Repeat use:** still satisfying at high frequency; variation only if predictable.
- **Discovery:** curiosity rewarded; required paths stay obvious.

Use product vocabulary—generic whimsy loses to clear neutral copy.

## Guardrails

Must not: block primary tasks; break platform or a11y norms; invent facts; play audio without consent; force unskippable loops; add heavy deps for a small beat.

Authored motion → [animate.md](animate.md). Respect SR, keyboard, touch, i18n, culture. Stop decorative loops offscreen. Match intensity to frequency and stakes.

## Verify

- Another product couldn’t reuse unchanged.
- Helps comprehension, confidence, motivation, or recovery.
- Still fast and obvious without flourish.
- Repetition stays pleasant.
- Muted, keyboard, touch, i18n paths OK.
- Feels like the chosen world.

Then `/ui-design polish`.
